Both phones work: tap them. Switch between the two features with the buttons.

Colour Gumamela

Bahay kubo wall

How it works

  • Each plant you've explored gets a colouring page (15 in all), opened from the plant's page ("Colour it") or from the bahay kubo.
  • Tap a colour, then tap a part to fill it. No messy brushes, so it's easy for small fingers and always looks neat.
  • Any colour is allowed: a blue gumamela is fine. It's for fun, not a test.
  • HANG IT puts the picture in a frame on the bahay kubo wall in My Garden. Tap the kubo to see your gallery.
  • First finished picture of each plant: +2 leaves.

Choices for later

  • A "real colours" hint button that shows the plant's true colours.
  • Patterns (dots, stripes) as extra paints bought in the Shop.

Binhi's story

How it works

  • When a child finishes the last plant of a world, a short story opens (4–5 pages) before the next world unlocks.
  • Three chapters: Backyard → Rice fields → Forest, plus an ending when everything is done.
  • Each page has one picture and 1–2 short sentences. Read-aloud reads it and highlights each word.
  • Stories stay on the Profile page ("My stories") to read again any time.

What's in this sample

  • The end of Chapter 1: a breeze carries Binhi from the backyard to the rice fields.
  • The real pictures would use the app's world scenes and Binhi drawings.
