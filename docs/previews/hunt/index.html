How it works

  • Opens from the Quiz tab (or a card on Home). Three hunts: At home, Outside, At school, 6 things each.
  • Each thing has a small drawing and a hint from Binhi when you tap it.
  • Each find: +1 leaf, a FOUND stamp, the vine grows. Finish a hunt: a sticker + 5 leaves.
  • Works fully offline: no camera, no photos saved, no location.
  • A safety note stays on top: go with a grown-up, look don't pick, don't eat plants.
